About me

Hi, I'm Marina.

I've been cleaning homes on Cape Cod for more than five years. I started with a couple of families in Hyannis who passed my name along to their neighbors, and that's still how most of my work comes to me today.

I work alone, on purpose. It means I can only take a limited number of homes — but it also means the person who learns where you keep your things and how you like your kitchen left is the same person who walks in the next time.

A lot of the houses I care for are summer homes and rentals, so I'm used to keys, lockboxes, cleaning schedules that shift with the season, and owners who are three states away and need to know it's handled.

— Marina

Questions

Before you call

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes, I bring everything — products, cloths and vacuum. If you'd rather I use your own products, or something specific because of allergies, pets or surfaces, just leave them out and let me know.

Do I need to be home?

Not at all. Most of my clients aren't. A key, a lockbox or a door code all work, and I'll let you know when I've arrived and when I've finished.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size and condition of the home and how often I come. Most homes on the Cape fall in a range I can give you in about two minutes on the phone once I know the number of bedrooms and bathrooms. The estimate is always free.

Are you okay with pets?

Absolutely. Just tell me their names and anything I should know — a door to keep closed, a cat who bolts, a dog who needs a hello first.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Life happens. Just let me know at least 24 hours ahead when you can, and we'll find another day. No fees for the occasional change.

Do you do same-day turnovers for rentals?

Yes, that's a big part of my summer. Give me your checkout and check-in times and I'll tell you honestly whether I can fit the window — I'd rather say no than leave a guest walking into a half-ready house.

How do I book?

Call or send a WhatsApp message. Tell me your town, how many bedrooms and bathrooms, and what kind of cleaning you're after. I'll come back to you the same day.
